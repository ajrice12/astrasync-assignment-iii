Summary

AstraSync must choose a contract that covers its $120,000 monthly delivery cost while balancing profit and uncertainty. I discount 18 months of profit at 1.5% per month and simulate 1,000,000 triangular client-savings outcomes for the RFP gain-share. Expected present values are $501,521.95 for A, $506,713.29 for B, and $509,752.90 for C. I recommend option C because it has the highest average payoff. Its small advantage depends on the 40% chance of winning, with a 60% chance of no contract.

Client savings and gain-share

Savings follow Triangular(low $3.2M, mode $5.6M, high $12.8M), conditional on winning. The share is $0 below $4M; 20% of excess above $4M through $6M; $400,000 + 40% of excess above $6M through $8M; and $1.2M + 60% of excess above $8M beyond $8M.

Mean reward: $1,051,370.42; median: $767,954.05. The right tail pulls the mean above the median. These are undiscounted rewards conditional on winning, rather than total contract payoffs. Monte Carlo standard error for C: $281.03.

Sensitivity analysis

Bonus probability and RFP win probability directly control the expected value of the uncertain offers. The charts vary each from 0% to 100% while holding all other inputs constant.

Bonus probability: B beats A above 69.15% and becomes the best of all three above 70.50%, holding the RFP win probability at 40%.

RFP win probability: C becomes the best option above 39.76%, holding the bonus probability at 70%. The current 40% estimate is only slightly above this threshold.

Conclusion

I would choose C based on its average payoff. Its lead over B is $3,039.60, while losing the RFP produces no contract with 60% probability. If AstraSync prioritizes a guaranteed contribution, A offers $501,521.95 with no bonus or award uncertainty. Validate the win estimate and savings forecast before making the final commercial decision.

Assumptions: month-end operating costs and payments; gain-share at month 18; no unprovided bid costs, taxes, or opportunity costs; delivery cost fixed at $120,000/month; RFP savings are conditional on award. Sampling variability causes C to differ slightly across seeds and simulation counts.
